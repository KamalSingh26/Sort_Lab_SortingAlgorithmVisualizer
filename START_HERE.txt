SORT LAB — SORT YOUR INPUT AND COMPARE ALGORITHMS

1. Extract the ZIP using Extract All.
2. Open the Sorting_Algorithm_Visualizer folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
4. Enter numbers, for example: 4, 3, 2, 1
5. Choose Bubble Sort, Selection Sort, Insertion Sort, Merge Sort, or Quick Sort.
6. Click Sort & compare.

You will see your input, the sorted array from your chosen algorithm,
a comparison table for all five sorts, and the best under the scoring rule.

Total operations = comparisons + array writes.
The lowest total wins, and ties are shown.
Merge Sort counts temporary-buffer writes as well as result-array writes.
This does not measure real execution time.

Input: 1–50 numbers, separated by commas, spaces, or newlines.
Negatives, decimals, zero, and duplicates work.
Number range: -1,000,000,000 to 1,000,000,000.

No installation, internet, server, or database is needed.
Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js together.
README.md explains the code and scoring rule.
PROJECT_NOTES.md contains project notes and 25 viva answers.
